About

Professionally, I'm a backend developer working in C#/.NET and T-SQL/SQL Server, with fullstack experience building UIs in Vue.js and React.

I'm a genuine C#/.NET enthusiast — it's my language of choice even outside of work, and it's what a good chunk of my personal projects are written in too.

Beyond that, I like building the kind of software I'd want to use myself: snappy, lightweight, and pleasant to live in every day. Most of that personal work lives on Windows — file management, shells, and window switching — with a constant eye on startup time, memory footprint, and the small interaction details that make an app feel instant.

Even on a modern, up-to-date Windows install, a surprising amount of the day-to-day experience still feels sluggish or half-finished, and I enjoy fixing that — quicker apps, sharper UI, and workflows that respect your keyboard and your time.
